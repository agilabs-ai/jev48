# Attribution and scope

Jev48 is an independent weekend reproduction experiment targeting the public behavior/interface of TypeSafe's Jev.

It is **not affiliated with TypeSafe** and does not claim access to Jev's proprietary architecture, training data, or RLCD implementation.

Jev48 intentionally uses public prior art. Its selected starting point is:

- repository: `Mapika/decider`
- pinned commit: `b08acf787d5d1f718a8c36c4677960f43772c7be`
- starting model: `Mapika/decider-2b`
- upstream license: Apache-2.0

If a Jev48 derivative checkpoint is published, it must retain all applicable upstream notices and model/dataset obligations.
